Your files never leave your device

Every PDF and image you add is processed inside your browser. We never receive, store, or see your files, their names, or their contents. The site’s security policy instructs your browser to block requests to any other server, and the app works with your internet connection switched off.

What we collect

Nothing about you or your documents. We use no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party scripts or fonts.

What our host sees

fold is served by [HOSTING PROVIDER]. Like any web host, it handles your IP address and browser details in order to deliver the site, and may keep short-lived logs for security and reliability. That is the only personal data involved in using fold. It is processed under our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), under a data processing agreement with the provider. See [HOSTING PROVIDER PRIVACY LINK] for their retention periods.

Files you export

Exported PDFs are created on your device and saved straight to it. We strip document metadata such as author and creator fields from the files we produce.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because we hold none, the answer will almost always be “nothing”, but you are welcome to ask. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

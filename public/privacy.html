Privacy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Your files never leave your device

Every PDF and image you add is processed inside your browser. We never receive, store, or see your files, their names, or their contents: Cornerfold’s code never sends them anywhere. As a second safeguard, the site’s security policy tells your browser to block the usual ways a web page reaches other websites, such as loading scripts or images from them or sending them data requests. And after your first visit, the app works with your internet connection switched off.

What we collect

Cornerfold itself collects nothing about you or your documents. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising, or third-party scripts or fonts.

Two kinds of limited personal data can still come up, and both are explained below: the technical details your browser shares with our hosting provider when it loads the site, and anything you choose to send us by email.

To work offline, the app saves its own code in your browser’s storage. That storage holds only the app itself, never your files or any information about you, and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Because Cornerfold tracks nothing, “Do Not Track” and Global Privacy Control signals from your browser have nothing to switch off. You get that result whether or not you send them.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to reply. Mail to cornerfold.org addresses is forwarded by Cloudflare’s email routing service, which handles it only to deliver it, to an inbox hosted by Google (Gmail), so Google processes it too. We keep correspondence only as long as we need it to deal with your message. We don’t add you to any list, and if you ask, we delete the conversation, including attachments.

The “Report a problem” link opens your own email app with a draft that contains Cornerfold’s version and the name of your browser. You can edit or delete anything in it, and nothing is sent unless you press send. It never contains anything about your documents.

What our host sees

Cornerfold is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, it handles your IP address and browser details in order to deliver the site, and may keep logs for security and reliability. Apart from email you choose to send us, that is the only personal data involved in using Cornerfold. It is processed under our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), under Cloudflare’s data processing addendum. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for how they handle this data and how long they keep it.

Check it yourself

You don’t have to take our word for any of this. Open your browser’s developer tools, select the Network tab, then add and export a file. You’ll see Cornerfold load its own files and nothing else: no request ever carries your document anywhere. You can also switch on airplane mode after your first visit and keep working.

And you can read Cornerfold’s source code itself, which is public on GitHub: every line that touches your files is there to inspect.

Files you export

Exported PDFs are created on your device and saved straight to it. Here is exactly what happens to the document information inside them:

  • When you merge, split, rearrange, rotate or convert images, the new PDF carries none of the original document properties: no title, author, subject, keywords, creator, producer, creation or modification dates, and no embedded metadata (XMP).
  • When you compress a single PDF without changing it, the title is kept, and the other properties listed above are removed.
  • When you add a JPEG or PNG photo, the hidden details it carries (such as the camera model, the date and time, and the GPS location) are not copied into the PDF. For JPEGs they are removed without re-encoding, so the picture’s quality is unchanged. Cornerfold does not look inside images that are already part of a PDF you add.
  • What’s on the pages themselves, including comments, highlights and links, is kept. Cornerfold doesn’t scan pages for hidden personal information, so check sensitive documents yourself before sharing them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU GDPR and the UK GDPR give you rights over your personal data, including the right to ask what we hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act gives similar rights once its rights provisions take effect in May 2027. Until then, we honour the same requests from anyone, wherever they live.

The only personal data we hold ourselves is email you have sent us, and we will answer any request about it. We don’t see Cloudflare’s request logs, but we will pass on any request about them and help where we can. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Security

Found a security problem? Please email security@cornerfold.org. We read every report and will credit you if you’d like. Our contact details for researchers are also published at /.well-known/security.txt.

Who is responsible

Cornerfold is run by Utkarsh Sinha, an individual developer based in India, who is responsible for this service under the GDPR and India’s DPDP Act. For any privacy question or request, write to hello@cornerfold.org.

Changes to this policy

If a future feature needs to process files on a server (for example, converting Word documents), it will be clearly labelled before you use it, and this page will be updated first. Every change to this policy is listed, with its date, in the release history.

Support Cornerfold

Cornerfold is free

Cornerfold at cornerfold.org is free to use. Every feature works without payment, a trial, an account, usage limits or watermarks.

Supporting it is optional

If Cornerfold has been useful to you, you can support its development with a voluntary payment:

  • Ko-fi: international, from $1.
  • Buy Me a Coffee: for supporters in India.

A support payment is a voluntary gift. It does not buy anything: no features, no priority, no support and no say in what is built. Cornerfold works exactly the same whether you pay or not. Payments, receipts and refunds are handled by Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee under their own terms.

Your privacy

These are ordinary links. Cornerfold loads nothing from Ko-fi or Buy Me a Coffee and sends them nothing. If you follow a link, their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

Other ways to help, all free

  • Star the project on GitHub.
  • Report a problem: hello@cornerfold.org.
  • Tell someone who needs it.

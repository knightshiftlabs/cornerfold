About Cornerfold

PDF tools that never see your files.

Why it exists

Most online PDF tools ask you to upload your documents to their servers: ID proofs, salary slips, contracts, medical reports. They usually promise to delete them afterwards, but you have no way to check.

Cornerfold takes a different route. Everything happens in your browser, on your own device, so there is nothing to upload, nothing to store and nothing to delete. You don’t have to trust a promise, because your files never go anywhere in the first place.

How that’s possible

Phones and laptops are now powerful enough to merge, split and compress PDFs on their own. Cornerfold uses that power instead of a server. It also means the app keeps working with your internet switched off, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking. That isn’t just a policy: there is simply no system that could collect anything.

Who makes it

I’m Utkarsh Sinha, and I build Cornerfold on my own, in India. Every change is listed in the release history, and the privacy policy explains exactly what does and doesn’t happen to your data.

About Cornerfold

PDF tools that never see your files.

Why it exists

Most online PDF tools ask you to upload your documents to their servers: ID proofs, salary slips, contracts, medical reports. They usually promise to delete them afterwards, but you have no way to check.

Cornerfold takes a different route. Everything happens in your browser, on your own device, so there is nothing to upload, nothing to store and nothing to delete. You don’t have to rely on a promise: the site’s own checks, and the ones you can run yourself, are on the Check our claims page.

How that’s possible

Phones and laptops are now powerful enough to merge, split and compress PDFs on their own. Cornerfold uses that power instead of a server. It also means the app keeps working with your internet switched off. Cornerfold has no upload system, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, so there is nothing on our side that could collect your documents. The only thing that sees your visit is our hosting provider, which handles the technical details needed to deliver the website, as the privacy policy explains.

Who makes it

Cornerfold is made by KnightShift Labs, an independent developer in India. Every change is listed in the release history, and the privacy policy explains exactly what does and doesn’t happen to your data.

Cornerfold is AI-coded. Most of its code was written by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, working with KnightShift Labs, who set the requirements, review the changes and decide what is released. It has automatic tests (listed on the Check our claims page) but has not had an independent security audit, and it comes with no warranty. Please check anything important before you rely on it. The code is public, so you can read it for yourself.
